🌾 WHEAT

Wheat Straw and Stalks

Wheat harvesting can leave straw, stalks and chaff. These materials can be collected and directed towards several useful applications instead of being treated only as waste.

Waste Generated Straw, stalks and chaff
Preparation Dry, clean and chop where required
Possible Pathways Mulch, compost, bedding and fibre products
Value Idea Farm input or raw material

⚠
Important Safety Note

Not every agricultural residue is suitable for every application. Always identify the material correctly, keep it free from contamination and use suitable equipment and technical guidance. Never burn unknown or contaminated material.

How agricultural waste
moves through the cycle.

A simple decision path for farmers to understand how agricultural residue can move from the field towards useful applications.

01
🧺

Collect

Collect crop residue after harvesting and keep it separate from plastic, stones, soil and other unwanted materials.

02
🔎

Separate

Separate different materials according to crop, moisture, size, fibre type and intended use.

03
✂️

Prepare

Dry, clean, chop, shred or crush the material according to the selected recycling method.

04
⚙️

Process

Apply a suitable technique such as composting, mulching, briquetting, fibre processing or controlled biochar production.

05
📦

Use or Sell

Use the finished resource on the farm or explore local opportunities for value-added products.

Turn a farm problem
into a resource system.

Agri-Reuse is an educational platform designed to help farmers and rural communities understand what can be done with agricultural waste and crop residue generated after harvesting.

The platform follows a simple idea: identify the residue, understand its properties, select a suitable technique, process it safely and turn it into something useful.

The objective is not to suggest that one method works for every farm. Different crops, moisture levels, contamination, equipment, local conditions and market opportunities must be considered.
